About

Hi, I'm Pranav! I'm a master's student at Carnegie Mellon studying machine learning, and I did my undergrad in applied math and economics at UCLA.

My interests are in AI reasoning, oversight, and alignment, and I am currently evaluating how humans interact with agents of increasing capabilities and the potential effects of that at various scales. I'm always interested in learning more about these topics and others; feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss or collaborate!

In my free time I enjoy going on hikes, playing chess, and solving puzzles.

Current Research

LLM Proactivity and User Reliance. With Grace Liu (PhD student) and advised by Professor Aarti Singh in the CMU Machine Learning Department, I'm working on benchmarking proactive behavior in LLM chatbots and agents, and identifying potential cases of misalignment with user goals through cognitive offloading.

Industry Experience

BlackRock, Aladdin Research (Summer 2026). I built a multi-agent research pipeline to generate investment research insights from unstructured data on the Aladdin platform. I then designed an LLM-as-judge evaluation framework to measure the verifiability and validity of those insights based on the data.
Chicago Trading Company, Metals Desk (Summer 2025). I spent the summer learning how to make markets in options and systematically adjust prices based on new information. On the metals desk, I analyzed millions of trades to identify features predictive of their profitability.
